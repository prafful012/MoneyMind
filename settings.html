Security & Privacy Transparency Notice

MoneyMind operates 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 LocalStorage. No bank passwords, card details, or financial numbers are ever requested or uploaded to any cloud server. Your data resides solely on this computer and browser profile. Remember to export regular JSON backups to keep your data safe against accidental browser cache clearance.
